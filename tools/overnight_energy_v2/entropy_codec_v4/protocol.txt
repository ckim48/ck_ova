Lossless quantized-unlearning stream: CPU diagnostic v4

Motivation: digital U transmission accounts for roughly 95--99% of expected
Hybrid communication energy in the saved round. Encode the ORIGINAL 8-bit
quantized integers and float32 scales losslessly. This changes payload size,
not U quantization accuracy. A fallback raw-packed representation and zlib
representations are compared; all actual header and checksum bytes count.
Three tests cover exact original-quantizer reconstruction at 2/4/6/8/12/16
bits, zero payload, packet truncation and dimension mismatch. Real round-trip
checks pass on all active clients in the saved round.

Protocol change: a client-wide stream is evenly split into ordered byte
fragments across the M wireless blocks. Digital fragments are no longer tied
to the same U coordinate block; the head reconstructs all U only after the
round. The original final model update already occurs after all blocks. This
is an explicit new protocol, not a silent change to the existing manuscript.
Original quantization bounds and decoded U are retained exactly.

Before head/power design, each active client reports its compressed stream
length in 32 extra control bits. The CPU audit charges a serialized broadcast
at the allowed maximum RF power over the weakest peer link (ideal channel
coding), counts control time and TX energy, and conservatively reserves the
largest client control-TX energy from every UAV battery budget. Hybrid and
phase separation use the SAME codec and length signaling. Mixed AirComp has
no digital stream and pays no unnecessary length signaling. Original total
communication deadline, peak power, battery and RMS envelopes remain in force.

Unmeasured costs: codec computation on UAV hardware and extra control RX
circuitry. Host encoding/decoding timings are reported as host timings only,
never converted to UAV Joules. The audit reports radio-plus-control-TX energy
and a break-even allowance for missing costs versus the uncoded setting.
It does NOT declare an end-to-end winner or insert these points into the
completed full-training energy frontier. A deployable comparison must account
for those costs and their delay. No free full-gradient geometry is assumed.

Prespecified CPU grid: 8-bit original quantization; original relative envelope
0.2 first, followed by stricter 0.1 and the previous loose candidate 0.6.
Hybrid and phase (all seven fractions) receive the same payload, quantization,
time/power/battery limits and joint-power solver. Saved strongest Mixed cases
remain visible. All failures remain visible. Only after evidence and costs are
validated should any new GPU trajectory be considered. Existing GPU queues,
including burst_joint_v3, remain untouched at this stage.
