Ground-truth video-rank distributions of all retained text queries at a common 400 kJ active-energy limit. Each method uses its last completed model within the limit; actual completed FL rounds and FU procedures are reported in progress.csv. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers use 1.5 IQR, and every outlier is shown. Lower ranks are better. The same retained query sets and full galleries are used throughout. Requests follow the original round-triggered schedule, so released and completed FU counts may differ. This is a retained-utility comparison under limited energy, not a comparison of equally completed or equally effective unlearning. Single development seed; no confidence intervals over training runs.
